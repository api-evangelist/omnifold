# Omnifold

> Omnifold is an AI supply chain planning platform for physical product companies — CPGs, manufacturers, distributors, and retailers. The company trains a custom AI model for each supply chain to forecast demand, optimize inventory, and surface revenue, margin, and cash that traditional planning systems miss. Omnifold competes with Kinaxis, Blue Yonder, and o9, and is positioned in the Forbes "Purpose-Built AI" tier of supply chain platforms.
